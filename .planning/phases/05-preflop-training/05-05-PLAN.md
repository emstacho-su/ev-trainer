## Root Cause Analysis

From code review of `PreflopTrainingSession.tsx` (lines 100-134):

1. **isActive logic is inverted** (line 124):
   ```typescript
   isActive: !isHero && !hasFolded,
   ```
   This sets `isActive: true` for players who HAVEN'T acted yet. Should be: players who HAVE acted should be inactive (dimmed).

2. **Bet display logic incomplete** (lines 111-119):
   - Only shows bets for players who have acted
   - Doesn't show bets for players who haven't acted yet (but should show blinds for SB/BB who haven't acted)
   - The condition `!isHero` (line 115) excludes all hero scenarios, even blind bets

3. **Action labels created but may not be passed correctly** (line 129):
   - ActionLabel is set only when `action` exists
   - For players who haven't acted, no label (correct)
   - But for players who HAVE acted, label should always display

## Expected Behavior

**Player states:**
- **Hero (hasn't acted):** Blue ring, cards showing, no bet display, no action label
- **Villain who hasn't acted:** Avatar + card backs, stack, blind bet chips (if SB/BB), no action label, normal opacity
- **Villain who checked:** Avatar + card backs, stack, blind bet chips showing, "Check" label, normal opacity (not dimmed)
- **Villain who called:** Avatar + card backs, stack, chips showing call amount, "Call" label, normal opacity
- **Villain who raised:** Avatar + card backs, stack, chips showing raise amount, "Raise" label, normal opacity
- **Villain who folded:** Avatar only (no cards), stack, no chips, "Fold" label in red, dimmed opacity

**isActive meaning:**
- `isActive: true` should mean "player is still in the hand" (not folded)
- `isActive: false` should mean "player has folded" (will be dimmed)

**Current bug:**
- `isActive` is being used to mean "hasn't acted yet" instead of "hasn't folded"
- This causes wrong opacity and wrong card display
